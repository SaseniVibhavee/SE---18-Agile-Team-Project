03 The Attesta Loop

We aren’t the first tools.
We close the loop.

Attesta keeps students in class. A fingerprint becomes a record. The record shows who is slipping. And that warning turns into real help — before a missed week becomes a dropped degree.

  1. 01

    PROVE

    A finger on glass.

    The sensor checks your fingerprint on the device in under 0.4 seconds. No screenshots, no shared links, no signing in for a friend. You have to be there.

  2. 02

    RECORD

    Offline by design.

    The device saves up to 512 check-ins on its own memory. If the Wi-Fi drops, scans keep working and sync when it's back.

  3. 03

    REVEAL

    Who is slipping.

    Lecturers see attendance by class and group, with at-risk students flagged early — weeks before a missed module becomes a drop-out.

  4. 04

    RECOVER

    Catch-up, grounded.

    The AI tutor only uses your uploaded lecture material. It asks what you know, explains what you missed, and passes you to your lecturer if you're still stuck.
